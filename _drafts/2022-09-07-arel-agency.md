# ThinkPrivacy is now part of The Arel Agency.

Since launching ThinkPrivacy in 2018, originally under the name RealPrivacy, we have accomplished so much we set out to do, which was to help bring privacy focused products and services to everyday internet users. Yet, one of the best accomplishments was the people we met along the way. 

We started getting asked more and more for help picking and choosing not only for individuals, but businesses and organizations who knew they could do better.

**So what does this mean for ThinkPrivacy?**

Immediately, nothing will change. We launched arel.agency today, and for now, both sites will continue to exist independently. However, our focus will remain on growing our new digital and creative agency and grow that new business venture. 

Over time, the ThinkPrivacy recommendations will make their way over to our agency site, and you will still be able to access the most modern and privacy friendly recommendations that we trust. The same recommendations we will help our clients enjoy. 

For us, this isn't the end of ThinkPrivacy, but the evolution of it. With Prism.Break, PrivacyGuides, and TechLore, we don't feel the need to compete in a great market of privacy lists and guides. Instead, we want believe our next step is helping connect the world of user and product.

In the coming weeks and months, you can expect to see some assets begin to rebrand, and eventually, the ThinkPrivacy site reloacting.

Until then, we will continue to ensure our lists of recommendations are maintained. We are also open to the idea of merging ThinkPrivacy with other privacy sites and or selling the brand itself to those who are looking to carry on its legacy.

**So what is The Arel Agency?**

[The Arel Agency](https://www.arel.agency) was founded by Dan Arel, a freelance journalists, author, privacy expert, and communications professional. Arel originally founded ThinkPrivacy was a passion project that focused on connecting users to privacy respecting products. ThinkPrivacy continued to grow but needed a new direction. Taking his talents in his professional life, and his passion for privacy and technology, The Arel Agency was born.
